## Writing voice

- Clear, practical, confident, and collaborative.
- Describe what was coordinated, supported, reviewed, or implemented.
- Use specific outputs and responsibilities rather than inflated adjectives.
- Separate professional employment, internships, volunteering, concepts, prototypes, and learning projects.
- Never claim PMP, advanced project-management software, formal English certification, or completed product status unless evidence is available.
